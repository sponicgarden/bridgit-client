1. Parties

This agreement is between Maple & Lane Lettings on behalf of the owner ("Landlord") and you ("Tenant") for Flat 4B, 18 Orchard Street, including the storage cage in the basement.

2. Term

The tenancy runs for 12 months from 1 December 2026. After that it continues month to month until either side gives notice.

3. Rent

Rent is €1,450 per month, paid by bank transfer on or before the 1st. Rent can rise once a year by no more than the published inflation index.

4. Deposit

The deposit of €2,900 is held by an approved deposit scheme and returned within 14 days of the end of the tenancy, less agreed deductions.

Flagged: deductions include "professional cleaning" at a fixed €250 whatever the condition. You can ask to strike this.

5. Repairs

The Landlord repairs the structure, heating, hot water and appliances supplied. The Tenant reports problems promptly and allows access with 24 hours' notice.

6. Pets

One cat or small dog is allowed with written consent, which will not be unreasonably refused.

7. Subletting

The Tenant may not sublet the whole flat. Short stays by guests of up to 14 nights a year are fine.

Flagged: a €95 "admin fee" applies to every renewal. Most agents in the city no longer charge this.

8. Ending the tenancy

After the first 6 months either side may end the tenancy with 2 months' written notice. The flat is returned clean, with the keys and fobs.

9. Signatures

By signing, the Tenant confirms they have read and agree to all clauses above.
