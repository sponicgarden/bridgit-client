What runs where

  • Timer → camera → free local change check → Clef yes/no on changed photos → episode tally on the phone → watch.matched event and, for alert situations, an urgent owner notice.
  • The phone expands situation into the tuned question, the tally (track = the situation id, or "situation" for own words) and the alert rule; unchanged photos reuse the last answer for free.

Controller integration

  • Optional: read GET /v1/watches/{id}/tally to archive day totals; the card and the count work with no agent.
  • Pick the situation with the owner: a library id from GET /v1/situations or the owner's own words.

Only Clef photo checks leave the phone (model left out, so Clef is the default for a camera watch); direct or relay transport still requires a running reachable phone.

Boundaries

  • A count is sampled photos judged by a model, not continuous coverage or proof: gaps, a locked camera or the thing being out of frame are unknown, not zero.
  • It does not diagnose health or detect fire: a smoke or boiling-over count is a nudge, not a safety alarm.

Lifecycle

start

POST /v1/watches with the start example; retain the returned watch_id. Then render/post the card with resource_id.

update

GET /v1/watches/{id}/tally first. To change the situation or timing, archive the tally, DELETE the old watch, POST the revised body. No watch PATCH exists.

pause

POST /v1/watches/{id}/pause with {"paused":true}; resume with false. Confirm user_paused from the returned metadata. Expiry is unchanged.

stop

DELETE /v1/watches/{id}; confirm deleted equals the saved id and watch.ended reason deleted. Archive needed tally BEFORE deletion. Cancel the interaction separately.

Failures and costs

  • Own words that look like an id (lowercase_with_underscores) but aren't in the library are refused: describe it with spaces instead.
  • A busy or locked camera is a gap; poor light, glare and odd angles cause misses and false counts. Aim the camera and try a known example first.
  • Clef photo checks from the phone's model budget: at most max_paid_per_hour (30 here, under the 50-an-hour limit some phones have) an hour; unchanged pictures reuse the last answer for free. Read GET /v1/limits and lower the cap or lengthen interval_s for long runs.

Extensions

  • Send {"situation": {"describe": "...", "alert": true}} to count own words and notify the owner when one starts.
  • Add callback_when on the situation id, or rules, to reach a controller or show other notices; min and count_only_while tune what counts.
